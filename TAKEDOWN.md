# Takedown and removal requests

We publish facts that colleges file themselves, federal data, and our own derived tables. We do not want to host
anything we shouldn't. This is how a request is handled.

## Who can ask
- A **college** whose numbers are wrong: use the correction template; we check against your filing and IPEDS and fix.
- A **ranking publisher** that would rather not be credited on college pages: ask, and your ranks come down from every
  page and file within five business days.
- A **student** whose public post is summarised on a college page: send the link to the post; the summary is removed
  within five business days, no questions asked.
- A **copyright owner**: send a DMCA notice (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)) to hello@opencollegedata.org with the subject
  "DMCA", or use the takedown issue template. Include the work, the page, your contact details, the good-faith and
  accuracy statements, and your signature. Counter-notices under § 512(g) are forwarded to the complainant and the
  material may be restored after ten business days unless a court action is filed. Knowingly false notices carry
  liability under § 512(f).

## What we do
1. Acknowledge within two business days.
2. Remove or correct within five business days; urgent personal-data requests sooner.
3. Record the request and the outcome in the log below (without personal details).
4. Repeat infringers lose the ability to contribute.
